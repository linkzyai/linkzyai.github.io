title: "Choosing photos for a business profile"
---

Photos on a business profile are often the first thing a potential customer looks at. Clear pictures of the premises, the team and finished work answer questions that text cannot, and they make a listing feel current instead of abandoned.

Good profile photos are well lit, in focus and honest about what the customer will find. A picture taken on a phone in daylight is usually enough, provided the subject fills the frame and the background is free of clutter.

Adding new photos regularly is more useful than uploading a large batch once. A steady trickle of recent work shows that the business is active, and it gives returning visitors something new to see each time they look.
