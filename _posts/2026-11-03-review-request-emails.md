title: "Sending review request emails after a job"
---

Email is the easiest way to ask a customer for a review once the work is done. A short message sent the same day, with one clear link, gets a better response than a long one sent a week later when the details have faded.

The message should sound like it came from the person who did the work. Using the customer's name, mentioning the job, and thanking them for choosing the business makes the request feel personal instead of automated.

One polite reminder a few days later is reasonable, and after that it is best to stop. Keeping a simple list of who has been asked prevents the same customer from receiving the request again after their next visit.
