title: "Writing useful service area pages"
---

A service area page explains what a business does in one particular town or neighbourhood. It earns its place only when it says something a visitor from that area would find useful, such as typical jobs there, travel times, or local rules that affect the work.

Pages that repeat the same paragraph with only the place name changed help nobody. Search engines recognise the pattern, and visitors notice it too, so it is better to publish three pages with real detail than thirty that are copies of each other.

Photographs of completed local jobs, a short description of each, and a map of the area covered give the page substance. They also give the owner a reason to return and update the page as new work is finished nearby.
