title: "How to ask customers for reviews"
---

Most satisfied customers are willing to leave a review, but few do it without being asked. The simplest approach is to ask at the moment the work is finished and the customer has just said they are pleased, while the experience is still fresh in their mind.

A short message with a direct link removes most of the effort for the customer. It helps to say roughly how long it takes and to thank them whether or not they follow through, since a request that feels like pressure tends to be ignored.

Replying to reviews matters as much as collecting them. A calm, specific answer to a critical review shows future customers how problems are handled, and a brief thank-you on a positive one shows that somebody is paying attention.
